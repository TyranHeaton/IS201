Extra-Curricular

Beyond the classroom and work, I have a deep passion for dance. I've been a dedicated dancer for over a decade, gaining rich partner and team leadership experiences that have shaped my character and developed essential collaboration skills for future work environments.

Dance

BYU Ballroom Dance Touring Company

I was a member of the BYU Ballroom Dance Touring Company, where I competed and performed at the highest level of collegiate ballroom dancing in the nation for three years.

  • Won three national championships
  • British formation team championship winner
  • Performed and toured internationally in Asia, Africa, and Europe
  • Performed for the King and Queen of Thailand and the Princess of Cambodia

Individual Competition

In addition to my team involvement, I also compete individually with various dance partners.

  • I compete in the Standard style of Ballroom - Waltz, Tango, Foxtrot, Quickstep, and Viennese Waltz
  • I compete in the Latin style of Ballroom - Cha-Cha, Samba, and Rumba
  • I've made numerous finals at competitions in these events over the years, including a National Championship
